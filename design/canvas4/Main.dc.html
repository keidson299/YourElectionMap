Tints, derived
--accent-tint — selected district fill · accent at 11% light / 18% dark
--accent-soft — selected list row · accent at 7% light / 11% dark
Boundaries are told apart by line style as well as colour — solid for the selection, 9/6 dash for county, 1.5/6 dot for school zone — so the map still reads in greyscale and to colour-blind viewers.
