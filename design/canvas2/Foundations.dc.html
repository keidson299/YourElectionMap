Three map-first directions

All three keep the layout you liked — a real district map behind a single address field — and vary the type, the palette and the shape of the search. Copy is identical across the three so the comparison is only about form. Primary action reads Find My Maps everywhere; candidates and dates are demoted to a secondary line.

Decided at design time
Visually hidden <label> on every field; submit through a <form> so Enter works.
Controls are 58–68px tall — over the 44px touch minimum at every width.
Both themes are real: every colour is defined on :root and only overridden for dark, never defined solely inside a media query.
The district chip in Meridian is sample text, not a real lookup.
Building this
Every direction is reachable in plain CSS — no library required.
